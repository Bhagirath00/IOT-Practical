Connections

1). LCD to Arduino Connection

Connect LCD RS pin to Arduino digital pin 12.
Connect LCD EN pin to Arduino digital pin 11.
Connect LCD D4 pin to Arduino digital pin 5.
Connect LCD D5 pin to Arduino digital pin 4.
Connect LCD D6 pin to Arduino digital pin 3.
Connect LCD D7 pin to Arduino digital pin 2.
Connect LCD VCC to 5V, GND to GND, and backlight to 5V via resistor.
