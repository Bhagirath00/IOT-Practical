<h4> Project description <h4>

Objective:

To demonstrate control of a servo motor for precise angular movement in IoT applications.

---

Components Required:

- Servo motor (e.g., SG90)
- Arduino board
- Breadboard and connecting wires
- Power supply (if servo requires more current)

---

<img src="https://www.electronicshub.org/wp-content/uploads/2015/05/Arduino-Servo-Motor-Circuit-Diagram.jpg" width="400"/>

Working Principle:

A servo motor uses PWM signals to rotate to specific angles. The Arduino sends pulses to control the position, allowing for automation in robotics or control systems.

---

Circuit Description:

1. Connect the servo's signal wire (usually yellow/orange) to Arduino digital pin 9.
2. Connect the servo's VCC (red) to Arduino 5V.
3. Connect the servo's GND (black/brown) to Arduino GND.

---

Applications:

- Robotic arms
- Camera pan-tilt mechanisms
- Automated gates or valves
